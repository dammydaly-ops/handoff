Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in plain words. Handoff builds it and explains each step so you always know what it does.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. The 14-day trial is free and needs no card. You can build one automation and test it before choosing a plan.

Which channels can I connect?

WhatsApp, Facebook and Telegram on every paid plan, Instagram from Growth, and more on Pro. Your plan sets how many you can connect.

What happens when I want to change something?

Ask for the change in a sentence. A new version is built, you test it, and then it replaces the live one. Your plan sets how many updates you get each month.

Are WhatsApp message fees included?

No. Meta charges its own fees for WhatsApp conversations. We show them separately so nothing is hidden.
